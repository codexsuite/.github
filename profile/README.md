---

Codex helps you plan the structure of a book, keep your research next to the draft, check every quote for copyright, and export a PDF ready for the printer, all in one app.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | Acts, chapters and scenes on a board. Move a scene and the manuscript follows. |
| **Research** | A knowledge base of people, laws, studies and stories that links itself into your text. |
| **Write** | A calm manuscript editor with your scene summary in the margin, and AI drafts that start from your plan. |
| **Clear** | Quoted words counted against your publisher's limits, with permission letters drafted for you. |
| **Print** | A live preview in a print template, preflight checks, and a print-ready PDF. |

Books can be grouped into a series that shares one style guide and set of settings.
